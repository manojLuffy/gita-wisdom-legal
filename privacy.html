Privacy Policy for Gita Wisdom

Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Gita Wisdom is operated by MKR Apps. Contact mo143on@gmail.com for support or privacy requests. Optional Google sign-in provides a user identifier, name, email address and profile image URL. We do not receive your Google password. Gita Wisdom is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly offer AI chat to children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

2. Reading and Local Storage

You can read bundled scripture, translations and guidance without an account. Preferences, meditation history and Guided Path progress and reflections are stored on your device. Guest bookmarks, reading progress and saved conversations stay in Guest storage. Signing in uses a separate account storage area and does not automatically upload older Guest records. Your operating system may include app data in backups according to your device settings.

3. Account Sync

When signed in, the app sends bookmarks and any bookmark notes, reading progress and Krishna conversations to Supabase for account sync. Synced records are linked to your account. These features require a network connection; an offline copy is stored on the device. Guided Path reflections and meditation history are not part of this sync.

4. AI Chat

When you request an AI reply, your question, selected language, relevant scripture or guide context and a limited recent conversation history are sent through our Supabase backend to Google Gemini API. The backend stores request identifiers, a payload hash, generated responses and usage records for allowances and safe retries. The app stores request copies locally for retry recovery. Do not submit sensitive or confidential information. AI replies may be inaccurate and are not professional advice. If you report a reply, the selected reply, reason and your account identifier are sent to MKR Apps through Supabase for review; your other messages are not included. Our Gemini API project is associated with active billing. Under Google’s paid-service terms, prompts and responses are not used to improve Google’s products. Google may retain limited records for abuse detection, legal or regulatory obligations under its terms. We store your confirmation of age and AI data use on this device for each account.

5. Notifications and Network Services

Daily reading and meditation reminders are scheduled on your device after permission and do not need a push token. In builds with remote announcements enabled, opting in can register a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, account identifier, platform, language and timezone with Supabase and Google. The current release has remote announcements disabled. Google sign-in and profile images, Supabase and Gemini receive the network information needed to handle requests, such as an IP address. The app has no advertising SDK or analytics/crash-reporting SDK.

6. Sharing and Security

We use Google sign-in, Supabase and Google Gemini to provide the services described above. We do not sell personal data or use conversations for behavioural advertising. When you use Share, you choose a receiving app and the content to share. Network services use HTTPS. The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or broad access to device files. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local records remain until you remove them using the app or remove app data through device settings. Account records and AI retry/usage records are retained to operate the account and support safe retries, until account deletion. Use Settings > Account > Delete Account to request deletion of the signed-in account and related cloud records. Successful deletion also removes that account’s local storage and request journals on this installation. Guest records, other accounts, device preferences, local meditation history and Guided Path records are separate and are not erased by account deletion. Copies on other devices and operating-system backups require separate removal. You can also email mo143on@gmail.com from the account email with the subject Gita Wisdom account deletion. We may verify ownership before acting. Service providers may retain security or backup records under their own retention terms.

8. Contact and Changes

Email mo143on@gmail.com for questions, deletion requests or concerns. Policy changes will be reflected in the updated date.
