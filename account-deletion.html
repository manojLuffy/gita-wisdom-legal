Delete your Gita Wisdom account

Gita Wisdom is operated by MKR Apps. Updated 27 September 2026.

From the app

Sign in to the account you want to delete. Open Settings, find Account, choose Delete Account and confirm. If the request fails, the app keeps your local records so you can retry.

Without the app

Email mo143on@gmail.com from your account email with the subject “Gita Wisdom account deletion”. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request. Do not send your password.

What deletion removes

The account and its cloud bookmarks and notes, reading progress, conversations, AI request and allowance records, reports and registered push devices are deleted. Successful in-app deletion also removes that account’s local cache and AI retry journals on that installation.

What remains separate

Guest records, other accounts, device preferences, Guided Path progress and reflections, and meditation history are stored separately on your device. Account deletion does not erase them. Copies on other devices and operating-system backups need separate removal. Providers may retain security or backup records under their own terms.

This page provides a request route; deleting or uninstalling the app alone does not delete your cloud account.
